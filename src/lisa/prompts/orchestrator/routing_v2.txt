Routes:

- general_enquiry:
  General questions, factual questions, casual conversation, or requests
  that do not report an operational issue and do not require specialized
  technical troubleshooting or explanation.

- issue_reporting:
  The user is reporting an operational problem, failure, incident,
  malfunction, or unexpected behavior that may require investigation
  or ticket creation.

- technical_clarification:
  The user is asking for a technical explanation, clarification,
  troubleshooting guidance, or understanding of a technical concept
  without primarily reporting a new operational issue.

- "Hi, how are you?" → general_enquiry
- "What is your name?" → general_enquiry

- "The firewall is dropping all traffic." → issue_reporting
